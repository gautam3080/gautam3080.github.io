About Gautam

I'm Gautam, a Unity game developer who likes the unglamorous middle of a game: the architecture, the systems, and the glue that makes them talk to each other. Over 3.5+ years I've worked across gameplay, bot AI, client-side multiplayer, UI, animation, and performance on Android, iOS, and Windows.

My latest work, Pongz – SG Mahjong, is live on both app stores. Before that I spent two years building Windows 3D tools in Unity, including a character creator I led development on. I work directly with artists, animators, sound engineers, and clients, and I'm happiest when a feature feels obvious to the player because the hard part is hidden.
